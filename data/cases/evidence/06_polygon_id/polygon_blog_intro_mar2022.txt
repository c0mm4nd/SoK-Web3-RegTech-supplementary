KEY TEXT (verbatim / near-verbatim quotes from the blog post):

Announcement date: March 29, 2022.

Positioning: Polygon ID is "the self-sovereign, decentralized and private identity for the next iteration of the Internet" powered by zero-knowledge cryptography.

W3C DIDs / VCs:
Mentions verifiable credentials (VCs) as an existing standard, noting that "VCs offer some degree of privacy with selective disclosure and ZK add-on, their limitations are in the expressibility and composability."

Iden3 Protocol & Circom:
"Polygon ID leverages the Iden3 protocol and Circom ZK toolkit." The announcement states both projects will be sponsored by Polygon while maintaining their open-source community character.

ZK-SNARKs:
The post references "zkSNARKs circuits," describing how Polygon ID leverages expertise to reduce complexity in using Circom 2.0 to compile these cryptographic constructions.

Compliance / KYC Use Cases:
The platform includes "KYC, KYB, attestation" as part of its organizational and business applications for trust management.

Wallet-to-Identity Binding:
The roadmap mentions a "Polygon ID Wallet app" and "Polygon ID Connect" as a public service platform to integrate access across user wallets and applications, enabling on-chain verification.
