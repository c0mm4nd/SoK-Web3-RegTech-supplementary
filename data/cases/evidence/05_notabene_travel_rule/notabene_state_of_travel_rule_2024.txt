KEY FINDINGS:

Publication: Q1 2024 (survey conducted October 2023 - January 2024).

Survey scope: 70 VASP companies across:
- Europe, Middle East, Africa: 45.7%
- Asia-Pacific: 30%
- Americas: 21.4%

Key compliance findings:
- 96% of companies now Travel Rule compliant or on track to compliance by end of 2024 (up substantially from 2023).
- 66% of VASPs enforce restrictions on withdrawals to self-hosted (non-custodial) wallets that cannot exchange Travel Rule messages.
- 23% do not allow any withdrawals unless a Travel Rule message can be sent to the beneficiary VASP (up from 8% in 2023).
- 19% of respondents in 2024 permitted withdrawals without Travel Rule message capability (down 49% from 37% in 2023).
- 40% of respondents adopt a risk-based approach to Travel Rule withdrawals.

Jurisdictional implementation:
- Notabene's regulator tracker shows 70+ jurisdictions with active or imminent Travel Rule requirements.
- Singapore, Germany, Switzerland, UK, Estonia listed as most-advanced implementing jurisdictions.
- US partial implementation via FinCEN guidance; EU implementation via TFR 2023/1113.

IMPLICATIONS FOR WEB3 REGTECH CODING:
- D11 Travel Rule Support: independent third-party-style survey shows 96% VASP-side adoption — establishes that Travel Rule capability is now a market requirement, not a differentiator. Substantiates Notabene (P25), Sygna Bridge (P26), TRISA (P27), 21 Analytics (P28), Shyft Network (P29), TransactID (P30).
- D03 Address Attribution: self-hosted wallet attribution is required for Travel Rule risk-based withdrawals.
- D12 KYC Integration: VASP-to-VASP counterparty identification.
- D02 Real-Time Risk Scoring: 40% use risk-based approach — implies real-time scoring capability.
- D24 SAR Generation, D25 Regulatory Reporting: VASP reporting obligations driven by Travel Rule.
