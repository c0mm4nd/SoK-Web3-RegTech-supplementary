Citation: U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission v. Changpeng Zhao, Binance Holdings Ltd., et al., Civil Action No. 23-cv-1887 (N.D. Ill. filed March 27, 2023). CFTC Press Release 8680-23.

KEY TEXT:

Date of action: March 27, 2023.

Filing court: U.S. District Court, Northern District of Illinois.

Defendants: Binance Holdings Limited, Binance Holdings (IE) Limited, Binance (Services) Holdings Limited, Changpeng Zhao (CEO), Samuel Lim (former Chief Compliance Officer).

Counts: 8-count complaint for violations of the Commodity Exchange Act (CEA) and CFTC Regulations including:
- Offering unregistered derivatives trading to U.S. persons
- Failure to register as a futures commission merchant (FCM)
- Failure to implement KYC/AML controls
- Failure to maintain customer-protection rules
- Causing employees to circumvent compliance controls (including VIP customer guidance to evade compliance via VPNs)

Substantive allegations (verbatim from complaint):
- Binance operated as an "unregistered derivatives exchange" offering cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ether, Litecoin, Tether, Binance USD) traded as commodity-futures instruments.
- "Binance and its CEO routinely broke American derivatives rules as the firm grew to be the world's largest trading platform."
- Defendants "secretly coached 'VIP' customers within the United States on how to evade compliance controls" and "directed its employees to spoof their locations through the use of virtual private networks."
- Samuel Lim (CCO) acknowledged in internal communications that Binance had "no choice" but to onboard customers from sanctioned jurisdictions including Iran, Syria, Cuba, Crimea.

Relief sought: Permanent injunction, civil monetary penalties, trading and registration bans, disgorgement.

Outcome: Resolved as part of the November 2023 DOJ global settlement.
