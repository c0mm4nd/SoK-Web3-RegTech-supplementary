KEY TEXT (from DOJ release summary and CoinDesk verbatim coverage):

Sentence and Financial Penalty:
- Prison term: 25 years
- $11 billion in forfeiture, including assets like a private jet
- Plus 3 years of supervised release

Charges Pleaded (per DOJ release summary):
Bankman-Fried was previously found guilty after a one-month trial on:
- Two counts of wire fraud
- Two counts of conspiracy to commit wire fraud
- One count of conspiracy to commit securities fraud
- One count of conspiracy to commit commodities fraud
- One count of conspiracy to commit money laundering

Underlying conduct described in DOJ release:
"Bankman-Fried, founder of cryptocurrency exchange FTX and trading firm Alameda Research, misappropriated billions of dollars of customer funds deposited with FTX, defrauded investors in FTX of more than $1.7 billion, and defrauded lenders to Alameda of more than $1.3 billion."

Verbatim from sentencing hearing (CoinDesk):
- Judge Kaplan: "Mr. Bankman-Fried never offered 'a word of remorse for commission of terrible crimes.'"
- Judge Kaplan: "When he wasn't outright lying, he was evasive, hair-splitting [and] dodging questions."

Victim impact:
Prosecutors alleged Bankman-Fried "stole $8 billion of customer money to fund venture-capital investments, real-estate purchases, political donations and more." Customer losses were estimated at "$8 to $11 billion."

Asset Recovery (verbatim from CoinDesk):
An attorney representing FTX creditors stated: "Sam and his team have been helpful to us" in recovering funds.

IMPLICATIONS FOR E3 CASE CODING:
- Paradigm: VASP (Centralized Virtual Asset Regulation). FTX was a centralized VASP; charges concern BSA-adjacent obligations (money-laundering conspiracy) and core fraud charges (wire/securities/commodities). The enforcement mechanism is classical intermediary-centric: indictment, jury trial, prison sentence, forfeiture order.
- Mechanism primary: KYT — the bankruptcy estate, DOJ, and academic analysts (Coquidé 2024) traced multi-token flows from FTX to Alameda. Address/Wallet Screening secondary because Kraken and Binance froze related addresses post-collapse.
- Lifecycle: Ex-Post (Investigative) — Chapter 11 forensic accounting, criminal prosecution, $11B forfeiture, multi-year asset recovery. No real-time control intercepted the misappropriation; the Ex-Post row's "regulatory reporting, audit trails" fits exactly.
