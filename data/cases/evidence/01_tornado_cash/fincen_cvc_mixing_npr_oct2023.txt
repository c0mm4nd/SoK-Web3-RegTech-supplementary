KEY TEXT:

Date of action: October 19, 2023 (NPR issued); comment period closed January 22, 2024.

Authority: Section 311 of the USA PATRIOT Act (31 U.S.C. Section 5318A). First-ever FinCEN use of Section 311 to target a "class of transactions" rather than a specific institution.

Designation: Convertible Virtual Currency (CVC) Mixing designated as a "class of transactions of primary money laundering concern" — particularly transactions occurring outside the United States.

Rationale cited: CVC mixing services enable money laundering and terrorist financing for entities including:
- Hamas
- Palestinian Islamic Jihad
- Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK) / Lazarus Group
- Russia-based ransomware operators
- Iranian sanctioned entities

Proposed reporting requirements: Covered financial institutions (domestic banks, U.S. branches/agencies of foreign banks, broker-dealers, MSBs, and virtual asset service providers) would be required to:
- Record and report specified information on transactions involving CVC mixing.
- Maintain records of CVC mixing transactions including counterparty identifiers, transaction amounts, wallet addresses, and timestamps.
- Report within 30 days of detecting CVC-mixing-related transactions.

Mixing-service definition (broad): Any service combining or pooling CVC from multiple parties to obscure source/destination/amount; includes both centralized and decentralized mixers, anonymity-enhanced cryptocurrency protocols, and on-chain privacy pools.

IMPLICATIONS FOR WEB3 REGTECH CODING:
- D16 Mixer Detection: this NPR makes mixer detection a regulatory mandate, not just a best practice — substantiates Chainalysis (P01), Elliptic (P02), TRM Labs (P04), CipherTrace (P03), Crystal (P08), Merkle Science (P05), AnChain.AI (P20).
- D24 SAR Generation and D25 Regulatory Reporting: mandatory CVC-mixing reporting creates demand for SAR-generation tooling — substantiates NICE Actimize (P38), Fenergo (P39), ComplyAdvantage (P35, P41), Refinitiv (P36), LexisNexis (P37).
- D15 Privacy Coin Analysis: broad mixer definition includes "anonymity-enhanced cryptocurrency protocols" — supports privacy-coin analytics demand.
- D01 Transaction Monitoring, D22 Real-Time Alerting: VASPs must detect mixing in real time.
