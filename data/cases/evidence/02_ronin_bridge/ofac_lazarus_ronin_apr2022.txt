KEY TEXT:

Date of action: April 14, 2022.

Sanctioned addresses: OFAC added an Ethereum address (0x098B716B8Aaf21512996dC57EB0615e2383E2f96) — containing the proceeds of the March 23, 2022 Ronin Bridge hack — to the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list under Lazarus Group's existing entry. Lazarus Group is a DPRK state-sponsored hacking organization originally designated under E.O. 13722 in September 2019.

Hack details:
- Ronin Network (an Ethereum-linked sidechain serving the Axie Infinity blockchain game) suffered a $625 million USD-equivalent hack on March 23, 2022.
- 173,600 ETH and 25.5M USDC drained via compromised validator keys.
- Largest known cryptocurrency heist in history at time of designation.

Treasury statement: "The FBI, in coordination with Treasury and other U.S. Government partners, will continue to expose and combat the DPRK's use of illicit activities — including cybercrime and cryptocurrency theft — to generate revenue for the regime." OFAC's update characterized this as part of the Biden administration's "persistent engagement vision" for confronting DPRK financially-motivated hacking.

Attribution methodology: The address attribution was made via Chainalysis, which the FBI publicly credited for linking the wallet to the Ronin theft. (This is a third-party attestation that Chainalysis serves as the FBI's blockchain forensics partner.)

IMPLICATIONS FOR WEB3 REGTECH CODING:
- D03 Address Attribution: Chainalysis (P01) directly named/credited by FBI/Treasury for linking Ronin Bridge stolen funds to Lazarus Group — strongest third-party regulator attestation in the dossier.
- D05 Cross-Chain Tracking: Ronin Bridge is an Ethereum sidechain bridge — demand for cross-chain forensics. Substantiates Chainalysis (P01), Elliptic (P02), TRM Labs (P04), Merkle Science (P05).
- D04 Multi-Hop Tracing: tracing stolen funds through subsequent mixer use (Tornado Cash) required multi-hop chain-of-custody analysis.
- D25 Regulatory Reporting: VASPs must block transactions involving the listed SDN address — substantiates D25 across all screening/monitoring vendors.
