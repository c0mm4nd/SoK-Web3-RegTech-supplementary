KEY TEXT (verbatim / near-verbatim from the blog post):

Fund Movement Tracing:
The stolen funds were transferred to the attacker's wallet 0x098B716B8Aaf21512996dC57EB0615e2383E2f96. Merkle Science documented the following distribution:
- 3,749.92 ETH (~$12.82M) moved to exchange E2
- 1,219.961 ETH (~$4.17M) sent to exchange E4
- 0.99 ETH (~$3,400) transferred to exchange E3
- ~$5M in remaining crypto sent to unidentified wallets

Approximately "$21 million in ETH has already been moved out of the attacker's wallet."

OFAC Designation:
On April 14, 2022, the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control designated the attacker's wallet, which "contains funds stolen in the Ronin security breach." At publication, the wallet held 138,433.136 ETH valued near $402 million.

Attribution and Investigation:
The FBI linked the breach to Lazarus, the "North Korean state-sponsored" hacking group. The U.S. Treasury stated this group is "controlled by North Korea's primary intelligence agency, the Reconnaissance General Bureau (RGB)."
