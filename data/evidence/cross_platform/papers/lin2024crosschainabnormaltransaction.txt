ABSTRACT (synthesized from publisher landing — verbatim text limited):

"Cross-chain technology enables interoperability between heterogeneous blockchain networks but introduces new security risks. Existing abnormal transaction detection methods focus on single-chain settings, which limits their effectiveness in cross-chain contexts. In this paper, we propose a graph-based multi-model fusion approach for cross-chain abnormal transaction detection. We construct cross-chain transaction graphs and apply multiple graph neural network models (including GCN, GraphSAGE, and GAT) whose outputs are fused via an attention-based ensemble. Experiments on real cross-chain transaction datasets demonstrate that our approach outperforms single-model baselines for detecting abnormal transactions involving bridges and mixing services."
