KEY TEXT:

Publication: Forbes Fintech 50, 2024 edition.

Crypto firms on the 2024 list: Three out of 50 — Chainalysis, Fireblocks, Gauntlet.

Per-company details (Forbes profiles, as reported):

Chainalysis (P01):
- Blockchain analytics firm specializing in tracking crypto transactions.
- New York City headquartered.
- Funding: $535 million raised.
- Valuation: $8.6 billion (May 2022).
- Headcount: cut 15% in October 2023, plus 5% earlier in 2023.
- Forbes recognition rationale: dominant in law-enforcement & financial-institution adoption of crypto analytics.

Fireblocks:
- Industry leader in crypto safekeeping (digital asset custody, transfer, settlement).
- New York headquartered.
- Funding: $1 billion raised.
- Valuation: $8 billion (January 2022).
- Forbes recognition rationale: institutional custody platform with bank-grade controls.
- NOTE: Fireblocks is more of a custody/MPC platform than a RegTech vendor; not directly mapped to a SoK platform but supports adjacent compliance use cases.

Gauntlet (P17):
- Pioneering platform for risk management and simulation modeling for DeFi protocols.
- Forbes recognition rationale: simulation-based DeFi risk modeling for major protocols (Aave, Compound, MakerDAO, Uniswap).

Notable absences (from earlier Forbes lists but not 2024): TRM Labs (P04) — was on 2022 Forbes Fintech 50; not selected for 2024. Merkle Science (P05) — not on 2024 list.
