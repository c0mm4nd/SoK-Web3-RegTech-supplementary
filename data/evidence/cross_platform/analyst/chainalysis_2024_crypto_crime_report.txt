KEY FINDINGS (verbatim quotes from Chainalysis 2024 Crypto Crime Report introduction):

Publication: 2024 Crypto Crime Report.

Total illicit volume (2023, the report year): "$24.2 billion" received by illicit cryptocurrency addresses. Chainalysis notes this is a lower bound — historical revisions typically roughly double figures once additional illicit addresses are identified.

Stablecoin shift: "Stablecoins now accounting for the majority of all illicit transaction volume" — replacing Bitcoin's historical dominance. Ransomware and darknet markets remain Bitcoin-dominated.

Crime categories that declined:
- Scamming revenue: down 29.2%
- Stolen funds (hacks): down 54.3% (largely due to fewer DeFi hacks)

Crime categories that grew:
- Ransomware revenue: increased (specific percentages quoted elsewhere)
- Darknet market revenue: rebounded after 2022 Hydra shutdown
- Sanctions-related transactions: 61.5% of illicit transaction volume (~$14.9 billion)

Geographic concentration: Significant rise in DPRK-linked Lazarus Group attributable hacks.

Methodology disclosure: Estimates use Chainalysis's address-attribution database; categories include illicit addresses (darknet, ransomware, scams, terror finance, stolen funds, sanctions, malware).
