KEY FINDINGS (verbatim quotes from Chainalysis 2025 report and Block / Defiant coverage):

Publication: 2025 Crypto Crime Report (covering calendar 2024 data).

Total illicit volume (2024): "At least $40.9 billion" received by illicit cryptocurrency addresses, with the estimate likely to "rise to top $51 billion as more illicit crypto addresses are identified."

Year-over-year crime trends (Chainalysis quoted):
- Crypto theft: rose ~21% YoY to $2.2 billion.
- North Korean (DPRK) hackers: responsible for "61% of crypto theft" — $1.34 billion stolen across 47 incidents.
- Stablecoins: "63% of all illicit transactions" (continued shift from Bitcoin).
- Ransomware payments: declined 35% YoY despite high attack volume.
- Less than half of recorded ransomware incidents resulted in payments.

Sanctions-related volume: continuing-rise category.

Major incidents named: DMM heist (May 2024, $308M, attributed to North Korean TraderTraitor / Lazarus by FBI using Chainalysis tracing).

Traditional-crime integration: "An array of illicit actors, including transnational organized crime groups, are increasingly leveraging cryptocurrency for traditional crime types such as drug trafficking, gambling, money laundering, human and wildlife trafficking, and violent crime."

Industry-context note: Chainalysis explicitly named by FBI in DMM heist attribution — third-party regulator attestation of Chainalysis's tracing role.
