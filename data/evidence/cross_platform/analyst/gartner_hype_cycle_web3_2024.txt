KEY FINDINGS (from publicly available Gartner summary content):

Publication: July 2024.

Methodology: Gartner Hype Cycle places technologies along five phases — Innovation Trigger, Peak of Inflated Expectations, Trough of Disillusionment, Slope of Enlightenment, Plateau of Productivity.

Technology positions (2024):

Approaching Slope of Enlightenment / Plateau:
- Decentralized Finance (DeFi)
- Blockchain Wallets
- Consensus Mechanisms
- Stablecoins (predicted "mainstream adoption within 2 years")
- Smart Contracts (predicted "mainstream adoption within 2 years")

At Peak of Inflated Expectations:
- Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)
- Metaverse

In Trough of Disillusionment:
- Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)
- Smart Contracts (scalability/security growing pains)

Key macro themes:
- "A major trend in 2024 is the increasing regulatory clarity surrounding blockchain technologies, with governments around the world introducing new frameworks for cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and DeFi to address issues such as money laundering, fraud, and consumer protection."
- AI-blockchain convergence: "The emergence of AI and collaborative tech partnerships opens new opportunities to combine blockchain and AI, enhancing trust and transparency in distributed networks."

Implied vendor implications:
- Blockchain analytics / compliance tooling is positioned within the "regulatory clarity" macro-theme — Chainalysis, Elliptic, TRM Labs, CipherTrace operate at the intersection of stablecoin/DeFi maturation and AI-blockchain convergence.
