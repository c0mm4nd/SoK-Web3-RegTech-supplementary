Citation: TRM Labs, "2025 Crypto Crime Report" (covering calendar year 2024). Published April 2025.

KEY FINDINGS (synthesized from TRM Labs report distribution + independent reporting):

Total illicit volume (2024): $45 billion (down 24% YoY from 2023). Represents 0.4% of total crypto volume (vs. 0.9% in 2023).

Composition of illicit activity (TRM categorization):
- Sanctioned-entity activity: 33% of illicit volume.
- Blocklisted addresses: 29%.
- Scams and fraud: 24%.
- Hacks and exploits, ransomware, darknet: remaining ~14%.

Scams and fraud detail:
- $10.7 billion sent to fraudulent schemes in 2024.
- Financial grooming ("pig butchering") scams: $2.5+ billion in 2024.
- Thousands of new phishing/investment-scam websites appearing monthly.

Ransomware:
- 5,635 ransomware incidents in 2024 (up from 5,223 in 2023).
- Largest single ransom payment: $75 million.

Drug sales: $2.4 billion in 2024 (+19% YoY).

Emerging tactics: AI-generated personas, DeFi-platform laundering, cross-chain bridge exploitation, abuse of new privacy protocols beyond Tornado Cash (e.g., Railgun, Aztec).

Sanctions focus: TRM emphasizes Russia/Iran/DPRK-nexus illicit-finance flows; cites Garantex (sanctioned Russian exchange) as a continuing case study.

Methodology: TRM Labs uses its own address-attribution database; consistent with Chainalysis classification but with somewhat different category weighting.

IMPLICATIONS FOR WEB3 REGTECH CODING:
- D03 Address Attribution: TRM Labs (P04) substantiated on D03 — TRM's blocklisted/sanctioned-address database is the foundation for the report.
- D10 Behavioral Analytics, D01 Transaction Monitoring, D02 Real-Time Risk Scoring: report demonstrates TRM operationalizes scam/ransomware/sanctions categorization.
- D05 Cross-Chain Tracking and D16 Mixer Detection: TRM's coverage of bridge exploits and new privacy-pool protocols.
- D15 Privacy Coin Analysis: extended privacy-pool coverage beyond Tornado Cash (Railgun, Aztec).
- D04 Multi-Hop Tracing: pig-butchering trace requires multi-hop chain-of-custody back to victim.
