KEY FINDINGS:

Publication date: September 12, 2023.

Report scope: Sample of approximately 30,000 Ethereum-based DEX liquidity pools, covering activity from September 2020 through August 2023.

Quantified findings (verbatim from Solidus Labs):
- At minimum $2 billion worth of cryptocurrency wash-traded on Ethereum-based DEXes since 2020.
- 67% of analyzed pools showed wash-trading manipulation.
- 16% of total trading volume on those pools was wash trading.
- Over 20,000 crypto tokens manipulated via DEX wash trading in three years.

Wash-trader categories identified by Solidus:
- Token deployers preparing rug pulls (artificial price discovery to attract buyers before exit).
- Speculators gaming airdrops.
- Exchange and marketplace operators inflating volume to attract investors and users.

Regulatory context (Solidus argues): Wash trading is well-addressed in traditional markets through trade-surveillance and self-trade-prevention systems; on DEXes it is just as detectable but the unresolved question is which regulator-side or market-participant-side actor is responsible for enforcement.

Methodology: Solidus Labs uses its proprietary HALO market-surveillance platform built specifically for crypto. (Note: this is what the SoK Platform Catalog records under P09 Solidus Labs.) The methodology is patterned on traditional-market surveillance (FINRA, SEC market-data analysis) adapted to on-chain transparency.
