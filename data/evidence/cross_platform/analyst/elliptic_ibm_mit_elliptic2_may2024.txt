Citation: Industry analyst coverage of Elliptic / MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab joint research, May 2024. Anchored on Bellei et al. arXiv:2404.19109 ("The Shape of Money Laundering").

KEY FINDINGS (industry analyst summary, distinguishing from primary academic paper):

Publication: May 1, 2024 (CoinDesk Technology section reporting); concurrent industry coverage by Security Boulevard, The Hacker News, Crypto-Talents.

Research collaboration: Elliptic (commercial blockchain analytics vendor, P02) partnered with MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab to publish open peer-reviewed research using:
- 200 million labeled Bitcoin transactions
- 49 million node clusters
- 196 million edge transactions
- 122,000 labeled subgraphs of Bitcoin clusters linked to illicit activity

Methodology: GNN-based subgraph classification rather than node classification — identifying the "shape" of money-laundering subgraphs (e.g., peeling chains, layering patterns).

Significance noted by independent commentators:
- "Elliptic2 dataset is orders of magnitude bigger than the one used when the team began using machine learning to detect money laundering with bitcoin back in 2019." (Coindesk 2024)
- Published at KDD Workshop on Machine Learning in Finance, August 2024, Barcelona.
- Open-sources the dataset for academic researchers — a notable third-party-relevant contribution.

Industry analyst interpretation (Coindesk, Security Boulevard):
- Demonstrates that AI/GNN approaches to Bitcoin AML can scale to industrial volumes.
- Identifies new typologies of money-laundering subgraph structure invisible to traditional heuristic methods.
- Suggests Elliptic is investing aggressively in ML-based compliance R&D to remain competitive with Chainalysis and TRM Labs.
