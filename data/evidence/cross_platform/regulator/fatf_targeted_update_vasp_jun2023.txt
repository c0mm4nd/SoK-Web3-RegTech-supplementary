Citation: Financial Action Task Force (FATF), "Targeted Update on Implementation of the FATF Standards on Virtual Assets and VASPs," June 27, 2023.

KEY TEXT:

Publication date: 27 June 2023.

Purpose: Update on country compliance with FATF Recommendation 15 (R.15/INR.15) and its Interpretive Note, including the Travel Rule. Updates on emerging risks and market developments.

Sample size: Findings based on 98 FATF mutual evaluation and follow-up reports since the revised R.15/INR.15 was adopted, plus a 2023 survey of 151 jurisdictions.

Key findings (verbatim from FATF report and confirmed by independent legal commentary):
- "75% of jurisdictions are only partially or not compliant with the FATF's requirements" on R.15.
- "Of the 151 jurisdictions that responded to FATF's 2023 Survey, more than half still have not taken any steps towards implementing the Travel Rule."
- Significant divergence between jurisdictions on registration/licensing requirements creates regulatory arbitrage risk.
- Identifies risks from DeFi, peer-to-peer transactions, NFTs, unhosted wallets, stablecoins.

Specific topics covered:
- Decentralized Finance (DeFi): FATF expresses concern about regulatory gaps where DeFi services may meet the VASP definition.
- Peer-to-peer (P2P) transactions: not directly subject to FATF requirements but pose risks via VASP off-ramps.
- Unhosted wallets: VASPs should apply risk mitigation when transacting with unhosted wallets.
- Stablecoins: same VASP definitions apply; mass-adoption stablecoins (USDT, USDC) raise systemic AML/CFT concerns.
- Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs): scope determination depends on whether they function as payment/investment instruments.
