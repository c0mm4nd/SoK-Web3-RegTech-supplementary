KEY TEXT:

Date of actions: June 5, 2023 (Binance); June 6, 2023 (Coinbase).

SEC v. Binance allegations: 13-count complaint alleging that Binance and Zhao operated unregistered securities exchanges, broker-dealers, and clearing agencies. Specific allegations:
- Operated unregistered Binance.US platform as a national securities exchange.
- Failed to register tokens including BNB, BUSD, SOL, ADA, MATIC, FIL, ATOM, SAND, MANA, ALGO, AXS, COTI as securities.
- Commingled customer funds and engaged in market-manipulative wash trading via Binance-controlled trading firms Sigma Chain and Merit Peak.
- Allowed high-value U.S. customers to evade restrictions.

SEC v. Coinbase allegations:
- Operated unregistered national securities exchange, broker, and clearing agency since at least 2019.
- Coinbase "made billions of dollars unlawfully facilitating the buying and selling of crypto asset securities."
- Coinbase Staking Program (XTZ, ATOM, ETH, ADA, SOL): unregistered offer/sale of investment contracts.
- Coinbase's Prime, Wallet, and trading platform conflated multiple regulated functions.

Relief sought (both cases): Injunctive relief, disgorgement, civil monetary penalties.

Outcomes (as of 2025):
- Binance: resolved as part of November 2023 DOJ global settlement.
- Coinbase: SEC dropped its case against Coinbase in February 2025 following change in administration policy.
