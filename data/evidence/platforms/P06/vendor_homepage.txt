# Scorechain Platform

## Core Products & Capabilities

Transaction Monitoring & Risk Assessment:
- "Monitor and score crypto transactions"
- Real-time transaction monitoring with customizable risk scoring
- "AI-powered blockchain risk intelligence engine" via Scorechain AI
- Configurable alert systems based on risk rules

Wallet & Address Screening:
- "Real-time crypto address risk checks"
- Wallet screening via API and platform access
- Sanctions exposure detection and counterparty identification
- Automated compliance checks using on-chain intelligence

Investigation & Fund Tracing:
- "Follow funds through blockchain networks" and "reveal hidden counterparties"
- Multi-hop tracing: "indirect exposure - including connections that are several hops away"
- "Visual transaction flow graph analysis" (Flux Analysis)
- Cross-chain analysis across 21+ blockchains

Regulatory Reporting & Compliance:
- "Real-time risk scoring, configurable alerts, structured transaction data, full counterparty exposure analysis, and audit-ready reports" for SAR/STR workflows
- KYA and KYT intelligence reports
- VASP due-diligence and entity directory (2700+ VASP assessments)
- MiCA, GDPR, FATF, OFAC, FinCEN alignment

Additional Features:
- Stablecoin and token activity monitoring
- Source of Funds verification
- "Digital Asset Intelligence" for asset ownership and activity insight
- MetaMask in-wallet protection integration
