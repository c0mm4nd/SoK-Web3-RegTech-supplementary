# Crypto-Assets, Wallets, Exchanges and 6AMLD - Main Content

**Headline:** Crypto-Assets, Wallets, Exchanges and 6AMLD

**Categories:** Knowledge & training | Regulation | Crypto

---

## Key Content Sections:

**Opening:**
"Cryptocurrencies or crypto-assets have gained a lot of popularity over the past few years. In particular, during the latter part of 2017 market capitalizations for many of the more prominent cryptocurrencies reached all-time highs."

The page explains that while cryptocurrencies have become more mainstream, they also "are also an attractive tool for criminals, who view their relative anonymity as a means to making their illicit activity easier to hide."

**Main Topics Covered:**

1. **Crypto-Assets Definition** - Distinguishing cryptocurrencies from e-money, discussing stablecoins, and regulatory classification challenges

2. **Criminal Uses** - "Bitcoin and other popular cryptocurrencies have been subject to a growing number of ransomware attacks" and sanctions evasion through "crypto-cleansing"

3. **5AMLD Impact** - Brought "cryptocurrency exchanges and custodian wallets within the remit of EU money laundering legislation"

4. **6AMLD Changes:**
   - New predicate offences including cybercrime
   - Extended liability to legal persons
   - Tougher penalties (prison sentences increased to four years)

5. **Future Outlook** - EU plans for "comprehensive and very clear" crypto rules by 2024
