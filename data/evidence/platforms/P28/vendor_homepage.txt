21 Analytics: Travel Rule Compliance Solution

Company Overview:
21 Analytics is a Swiss company founded in April 2020 that provides Travel Rule compliance software for Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs).

Key Products & Capabilities:

21 Travel Rule:
- "The only Travel Rule solution that guarantees GDPR-compliance"
- Supports VASP-to-VASP transactions and self-hosted wallets
- On-premises deployment with zero telemetry

AOPP (Self-Hosted Wallet Verification):
- "AOPP eliminates the manual element in self-hosted wallet proofs"
- Enables wallet ownership verification in one click
- No manual reviews required, eliminating transaction delays

Travel Rule Automation:
- Allows users to establish business rules to reduce manual work

AML & Compliance Features:
- Pre-transaction whitelisting for approval before asset transfers
- Support for over 2,800 digital assets via ISO 24165
- Encrypted email options for transactions with non-compliant VASPs
- User-based audit trails and SSO capabilities

Security & Certifications:
- ISO 27001:2022 certified
- DORA and NIS2 ready
- Built in memory-safe Rust
- External penetration testing by Swiss auditors
- "100% On-Premise. Zero telemetry, ensuring you retain absolute data sovereignty"

Business Model:
- Fixed pricing for unlimited transactions
- Peer-to-peer solution with no intermediaries
