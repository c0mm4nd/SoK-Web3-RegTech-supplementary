Veriscope by Shyft Network

Core Definition:
Veriscope is described as "The only frictionless Crypto Travel Rule compliance solution."

Travel Rule Overview:
The platform addresses the "Crypto Travel Rule and why the Sunrise Issue matters," relating to FATF (Financial Action Task Force) regulatory documentation requirements for virtual asset service provider (VASP) compliance.

Key Capabilities:

Discovery & Sharing:
- Enables discovery of counterparty VASPs and their addresses without requiring user information
- Facilitates VASP-to-VASP customer data transfer with security, without storing data on external systems

Transaction Support:
- Handles historic transaction lookbacks on broadcasts made before integration (addressing the "Sunrise Issue")
- Transaction broadcasts are immutable records

Identity & Security Features:
- No personal information is stored by Veriscope itself
- Secure PII transmission between VASPs prevents data mining of customer information
- Due diligence follows internationally recognized standards with attestation by DLA Piper

Ecosystem Scale:
Major crypto platforms already using or evaluating Veriscope include Binance, Bitfinex, Huobi, Gate.io, and others—representing over 60% of global transaction flow.

Business Model:
The solution operates on a "Pay-As-You-Go Model" with no setup fees, minimum charges, or ongoing licensing—only per-transaction fees.
