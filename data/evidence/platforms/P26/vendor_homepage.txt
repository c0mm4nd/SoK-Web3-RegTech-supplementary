Sygna Products and Travel Rule Capabilities

Hub:
"The comprehensive integrated AML compliance platform for VASPs. Get the industry's best 3rd party AML tools and services on one easy-to-use dashboard."

Features integrated blockchain analytics and sanction screening from multiple providers, plus Sygna Bridge Travel Rule protocol with interoperability support.

Bridge:
"The standalone API-based Travel Rule protocol for VASPs. Share AML-required Recommendation 16 transaction information with ease."

The solution supports over 3,000 virtual assets and has received independent assessment from Singapore's ACCESS association.

Gate:
"The browser-only integrated AML compliance gateway for VASPs. A manual version of Hub, requires no technical integration."

Offers the same integrated analytics and sanction screening as Hub, plus includes Sygna Bridge Travel Rule protocol with protocol interoperability features.

Key Credentials:
All Sygna products maintain "ISO/IEC 27001 certified" status, ensuring information security management system compliance and GDPR adherence for personal data protection.
