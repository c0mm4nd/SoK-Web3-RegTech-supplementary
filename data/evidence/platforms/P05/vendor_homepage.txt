# Merkle Science Platform

## Core Products & Capabilities

Compass - Transaction and wallet monitoring for compliance
- "Stay compliant with AML, KYC, and CFT regulations (predictive risk monitoring)"
- Prevents sanctions violations and detects scams using "behavior-based insights"
- Serves exchanges, financial institutes, dApps, wallets, and protocols

Tracker - Forensic investigation and fund tracing
- "Detect and trace illicit cryptocurrency activity and respond to hacks, scams, exploits"
- Covers "10,000+ assets and 200 bridges" with crosschain capabilities
- Targets law enforcement, investigators, and regulators

KYBB/Data Platform - Analytics and research
- Provides "AI-powered analytics for smarter decisions and deeper research"
- Enables "custom queries and access key data for large scale investigations"

Onchain Pulse - Ecosystem risk monitoring
- Delivers "proactive risk intelligence to strengthen compliance, mitigate threats"
- Flags "sanctions, darknet activity, and CSAM" with "risk scores for tokens"
- Includes "aggregate risk scores for VASPs"

## Key Features

- Behavioral rule engine for detecting suspicious wallets
- Crosschain transaction tracing across multiple blockchains
- Attribution capabilities (notably "best sanctions attribution for Russian and Iranian exchanges")
- Real-time onchain analytics and threat detection
- Predictive risk scoring
- Integration supporting new chains and tokens

## Training Offerings

Certifications in MiCAR compliance, advanced crypto compliance with AML/CFT focus, and crypto investigation.
