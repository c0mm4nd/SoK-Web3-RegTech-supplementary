Chainalysis KYT - Real-Time Transaction Monitoring

According to the Chainalysis Solutions page, Chainalysis KYT is described as:

"Monitor transactions in real-time" - the primary tagline for the product.

KYT enables users to "Ensure compliance and prevent illicit activity with continuous and real-time screening of crypto transactions." Organizations can "Tailor your risk settings, assess deposits and withdrawals, and audit suspicious user activity" through this monitoring capability.

KYT is positioned within Chainalysis's Crypto Compliance solutions suite, designed to help platforms maintain regulatory adherence while detecting potentially problematic activities as they occur on the blockchain.
