# Crystal Intelligence Platform Overview

## Core Products & Capabilities

Crystal Expert offers real-time transaction monitoring across 330+ blockchains covering 10,000+ digital assets. The platform provides "automated address screening, and sanctions and blocklist checks" for detecting suspicious activity.

Crystal for Compliance enables institutions to "reduce exposure to illicit funds" through entity risk analysis and comprehensive AML/KYC integration.

Crystal for Investigators supports law enforcement with capabilities to "trace funds and suspects, build cases" using interactive network visualization and transaction flow analysis.

Crystal Foresight delivers stablecoin monitoring to "uncover liquidity shifts early."

## Key Features

- Visual Investigation: Trace wallets across chains and "visualize transaction flows" with smart case mapping
- Entity Risk Analysis: Access verified data on 118,000+ entities with 360-degree risk profiles and sanctions screening
- Real-Time Monitoring: Instant alerts on suspicious activity with live transaction tracking
- Multi-Chain Coverage: Analyzes Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, Solana, and 326+ additional blockchains
- Data Scale: 210M+ verified transfers and 30M+ flagged transfers in their database

## Target Markets

Banks, payment providers, crypto exchanges, law enforcement, governments, and virtual asset service providers seeking compliance automation and financial crime detection.
