Crystal Intelligence Platform Overview

Core Platform Functions:
Crystal Intelligence describes its offering as "Advanced blockchain analytics for crypto crime investigation, compliance, and risk management."

The platform enables users to "Trace wallets & visualize transaction flows with interactive network maps" and "Flag risky flows instantly and prioritize threats."

AML & Compliance Capabilities:
For anti-money laundering requirements, the platform provides "real-time transaction monitoring, automated address screening, and sanctions and blocklist checks."

The system covers extensive blockchain coverage: "330+ blockchains and 10,000+ digital assets, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, Solana, Litecoin, and Binance Smart Chain."

Data Scale:
Crystal Intelligence maintains substantial verified data, including:
- 30M+ flagged transfers
- 118K+ verified entities
- 210M+ verified transfers

Primary Use Cases:
The platform serves "compliance and risk teams at financial institutions, crypto exchanges, and payment companies; law enforcement and government agencies investigating illicit activity."

Key capabilities include entity risk analysis to "Identify risky wallets & entities" and the ability to "Trace suspicious wallets across chains."
