# Forta: Onchain Security and Compliance Platform

## Core Product: Forta Firewall

Forta offers real-time monitoring and threat prevention for blockchain teams. The platform provides:

Key Capabilities:
- "Block malicious transactions before execution" (Security)
- "Screen against sanctions lists (eg. OFAC SDN) and other regulatory requirements" (Compliance)
- "Get alerts on key events asset depegs, anomalous transactions, oracle malfunctions, etc." (Monitoring)

Performance Metrics:
- ">99% recall" with "<0.0002% false positive rate"
- "< 10ms Latency"
- Uses "AI and simulations to identify malicious transactions"

## Integration & Infrastructure

The platform enables effortless integration "with no dev work needed" through partnerships with Rollups-as-a-Service (RaaS) providers including Gelato, Conduit, Quicknode, and Zeeve. Customers include Plume, Ink, and Celo.

## Network & Governance

Forta operates with a decentralized model powered by the FORT token. The ecosystem includes:
- Staking and delegation mechanisms
- Governance forum for community participation
- Data feeds and documentation

## Integration Partners

Major chains and infrastructure providers use Forta, with backing from prominent venture firms including Coinbase Ventures, a16z, and Blockchain Capital.
