# NICE Actimize Anti-Money Laundering Solutions - Content Summary

## Main Description
The platform is described as modernizing "KYC-AML programs by bringing together machine learning and domain expertise to help you better combat money laundering and terrorist financing."

## Core Solutions Offered
The page lists these specific AML capabilities:

- Suspicious Activity Monitoring (SAM)
- KYC (Know Your Customer)
- Sanctions Screening
- X-Sight Entity Risk
- Suspicious Transaction Activity Reporting (STAR)
- Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR)
- AML Essentials
- Xceed: Integrated FRAML

## Key Positioning
NICE Actimize emphasizes "entity-centric AML solutions, infused with AI and machine learning" that "optimize efficacy and accuracy" while providing "full AML compliance coverage and auditability."

The platform promotes an approach focused on "putting the entity at the heart of all anti-money laundering (AML) risk management processes."
