# Solidus Labs: Market Integrity Platform

## Core Products & Capabilities

Trade Surveillance:
"multidimensional surveillance to detect and prevent market abuse across any venue, product, or asset class." The platform correlates trading activity, on- and off-chain signals, funding flows, and social sentiment for real-time market abuse detection.

Transaction Monitoring:
"multidimensional screening of deposits and withdrawals across fiat and crypto," including AML compliance, fraud detection, and suspicious transaction identification.

Additional Solutions:
- Execution Quality: Quantitative best execution supervision for digital asset markets
- Stablecoin Monitoring: Full-lifecycle supervision across primary and secondary markets
- Token Monitoring: Smart contract scam and liquidity analysis
- Case Management: Unified investigation hub integrating multiple alert types
- Agentic-AI Workflows: Automated orchestration layer for compliance operations

## Platform Architecture

The HALO Platform serves as "the compliance control center for modern financial markets - unifying trade surveillance, transaction monitoring, KYC intelligence, case management, and agentic workflows."

## Target Markets

Solidus serves retail broker-dealers, global exchanges, institutional broker-dealers, prediction markets, market makers, custodians, stablecoin issuers, OTC desks, and regulators.

## Professional Services

Premium offerings include licensing, regulatory insights, model tuning calibration, and investigative support for digital asset compliance challenges.
