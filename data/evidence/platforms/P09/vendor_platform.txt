Solidus Labs HALO Platform: Key Capabilities

Core Description:
"HALO Platform — One Platform, Complete Compliance Coverage" offering "multidimensional, risk-based platform built from the ground up to safeguard modern financial markets."

Primary Functions:
The platform consolidates "trade surveillance, transaction monitoring, and investigation management within a single platform." It synthesizes information from multiple sources to help institutions detect potential market manipulation and investigate suspicious activity.

Key Capabilities:

Market Surveillance: HALO provides "multi-venue market inspection," enabling users to "visualize, replay, and reconstruct trading activity across multiple venues in a unified interface."

Detection Features: The system detects "spoofing, layering/quote stuffing, and socially amplified coordination" through "real-time detection" that combines order book analysis with behavioral analytics.

Configurable Parameters: Teams can "recalibrate detection thresholds, preview alert impact" via "zero-code interface" without requiring engineering support.

Risk Assessment: The platform builds "holistic, behavior-based profiles" and creates a "Universal Client Risk View" synthesizing cross-market data.

Target Users:
The platform serves "multi-asset broker-dealers," "crypto exchanges," "regulators," and "compliance teams scaling into digital assets."

Regulatory Focus:
HALO emphasizes "institutional-grade defensibility" and maintains "full audit trails" to demonstrate operational excellence across global jurisdictions.
