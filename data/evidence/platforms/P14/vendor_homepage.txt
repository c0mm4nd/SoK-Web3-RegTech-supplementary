# Hypernative: Real-Time Web3 Security Platform

## Core Capabilities

Hypernative offers "real-time monitoring, detection, and response" for blockchain threats across 70+ chains, protecting over $100 billion in onchain assets for 250+ customers.

## Key Products

Platform: Detects exploits with 99.5% accuracy, identifying threats approximately two minutes before the first hack transaction occurs.

Guardian: Provides "real-time transaction simulation, analysis, and enforcement" to eliminate phishing and fraud risks through proactive asset protection.

Screener: Delivers "real-time address reputation across multiple chains" for pre-transaction risk identification.

Wallet Protect: Alerts wallet users to malicious or risky behaviors in real time.

Firewall: Implements "transaction-level policy enforcement" to block specific malicious interactions without pausing entire protocols.

Sequencer/RPC Integration: Intercepts transactions before block inclusion.

## Target Markets

Solutions tailored for protocols, L1/L2 chains, asset managers, exchanges, financial institutions, payment providers, and wallet providers.

- 100% of 2023 exploits detected by Hypernative
- 98% of exploits detected before first hack transaction
- <0.001% false positive rate
- $2B+ saved to date
