## Primary Capabilities
"World-Check helps you meet your due diligence obligations and identify potential financial criminal activity with information from reputable public domain sources."

## Data Coverage
The solution includes politically exposed persons (PEPs), close associates, family members, state-owned entities, global sanctions lists, narrative and implicit sanctions, global regulatory and law enforcement lists, adverse media, and sanctioned securities.

## Additional Datasets

World-Check data is available through APIs, batch screening capabilities, World-Check One platform, World-Check On Demand, and World-Check Verify (AWS partnership).
