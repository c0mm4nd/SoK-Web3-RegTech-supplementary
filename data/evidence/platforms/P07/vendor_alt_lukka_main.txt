Lukka Data Solutions: Services, Products & Capabilities

Core Data & Software Solutions

Enterprise Data Management:
"Enterprise Data Management SaaS makes crypto financial reporting not only possible, but auditable." The platform streamlines middle and back-office operations for digital asset management.

Reference Data Products:
"Standardize, organize, and categorize over 1 million crypto assets with our Reference Data Products," including LDACS (Lukka Digital Asset Classifications Standards), asset mapping, marketplace data, and crypto asset scoring systems.

Pricing & Market Data:
Institutional-grade pricing infrastructure featuring normalized consolidated market data, VWAPs, custom pricing solutions, and digital asset reference rates designed for professional use.

Blockchain Analytics & Compliance:
Purpose-built tools including blockchain analytics SaaS, investigator platforms, and omni-tools for compliance oversight and transaction analysis across blockchain networks.

Compliance & Trust Infrastructure:
"Institutional-grade products are built within an AICPA SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II infrastructure," establishing audit-ready frameworks. The company maintains a dedicated Trust Center addressing data quality and reliability standards.

Industry Verticals Served:
Lukka serves funds/administrators, traditional financial institutions, crypto-native businesses, accounting professionals, and government regulators with tailored solutions for their specific operational needs.
