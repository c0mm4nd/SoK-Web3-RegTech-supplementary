ComplyAdvantage Solutions & Capabilities

Core Solutions:
ComplyAdvantage Mesh is described as an "industry-leading and trusted SaaS-based risk intelligence platform that unites global intelligence to beat financial crime."

AML Products:
The platform offers several specialized applications:
- Customer Screening: Part of the unified suite for managing risk across the customer lifecycle
- Company Screening: AML-focused screening capabilities
- Ongoing Monitoring: Continuous risk assessment functionality
- Transaction Monitoring: Software for monitoring transactions
- Payment Screening: Specialized screening for payment flows

AI-Driven Capabilities:
The company emphasizes that "AI powers every stage of the financial crime risk management lifecycle, from onboarding and ongoing monitoring to remediation and reporting."

Their Agentic Workflows feature claims to "resolve up to 85% of routine alerts autonomously while maintaining total regulatory defensibility."

Risk Intelligence:
The platform provides access to:
- Sanctions & Watchlists screening
- PEPs & RCAs (Politically Exposed Persons & Related Close Associates)
- Adverse Media screening

Core Positioning:
ComplyAdvantage positions itself as delivering "FinCrime compliance re-engineered for the age of AI" through an "AI-native financial crime platform with embedded agents."
