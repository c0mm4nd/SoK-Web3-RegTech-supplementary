ComplyAdvantage Products and Services

Core Platform:
ComplyAdvantage Mesh is described as "A cloud-based compliance platform, ComplyAdvantage Mesh combines industry-leading AML risk intelligence with actionable risk signals to screen customers and monitor their behavior in near real time."

AML & Screening Solutions:
The platform offers several screening capabilities:
- "Customer Screening" for individual evaluations
- "Company Screening" for entity assessments
- "Payment Screening" for transaction-level review

Transaction Monitoring:
The service includes "Transaction Monitoring" functionality as a dedicated application within the Mesh suite for observing account activities.

Ongoing Monitoring:
"Ongoing Monitoring" is listed as a core component for continuous customer risk assessment throughout their lifecycle.

Risk Intelligence:
The platform provides "full-stack proprietary risk intelligence designed for risk-based decisioning" covering sanctions, watchlists, politically exposed persons, and adverse media screening.

Key Features:
Notable capabilities include case management, automated risk scoring that is "fully configurable and dynamically updated," and agentic AI that can "Process 65-85% of profiles automatically without human intervention."
