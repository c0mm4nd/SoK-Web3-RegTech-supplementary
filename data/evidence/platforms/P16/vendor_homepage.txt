# Chaos Labs: Financial AI Infrastructure

## Core Mission
Chaos Labs transforms "complex, fragmented market data into decision-ready, institutional-grade intelligence." The platform has processed over $5 trillion in transactions and secured $50+ billion in assets.

## Three Main Products

### Chaos AI
Delivers "real-time, institutional-grade financial intelligence with built-in data verification and security." Features include:
- Real-time risk assessment models used by Jupiter, Aave, and GMX
- Instant hedge fund-quality research reports on protocols and tokens
- Personalized portfolio intelligence and recommendations

### Risk Management & Analytics
Equips protocols with comprehensive risk infrastructure featuring:
- "Model-based parameter tuning, backed by agent-based and Monte Carlo simulations"
- Scenario-driven risk insights and protocol-specific dashboards
- Live health monitoring with custom alerting capabilities

### Oracles
A risk-aware oracle stack that "treat[s] data as a risk variable, validating in real time, detecting manipulation, and triggering circuit breakers." Provides price feeds with real-time anomaly detection and institutional-grade reliability.

## Key Capabilities
The platform emphasizes real-time market intelligence, machine learning risk systems, institutional decision-making at scale, execution quality improvement, and continuous market data validation through anomaly detection.

## Notable Clients
Circle, PayPal, Kalshi, Paradigm, Kraken, Aave, Uniswap, Jupiter, and others.
